Grammar 1: Expressing Dates (BIG to SMALL) (Pg 141)

Rule: Always arrange time units from largest to smallest: Year -> Month -> Day -> Day of Week -> Time of Day.

🎯 Key Q&A Reinforcement: Asking Dates (Full Sentences)
Q1: Tā shì nǎ nián chūshēng de? (Which year was he born?)
A: Tā shì 1982 nián chūshēng de. (He was born in the year 1982.)
Q2: Jīntiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? (What date is today?)
A: Jīntiān shì shí yuè èrshíqī hào. (Today is October 27th.)
Q3: Nǐ de shēngri shì nǎ tiān? (When is your birthday?)
A: Wǒ de shēngri shì bā yuè èrshíyī hào. (My birthday is August 21st.)
Q4: Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ? (What day of the week is tomorrow?)
A: Míngtiān shì xīngqīsì. (Tomorrow is Thursday.)

⏰ Time Structure & Concept Table ("shàng" vs "xià")
Specific Time Order Formula:
[Day] + [Time Period] + [Hour] + [Minute]
e.g., Míngtiān shàngwǔ 9 diǎn (Tomorrow morning at 9 o'clock)
Comparative Table: Past/Present/Future Time Expressions
Time Unit Previous (shàng / 过去) Current (zhè / jīn / 现在) Next (xià / míng / 未来)
Month (yuè) shàng ge yuè (last month) zhè ge yuè (this month) xià ge yuè (next month)
Week (xīngqī) shàng xīngqī (last week) zhè xīngqī (this week) xià xīngqī (next week)
Semester (xuéqī) shàng xuéqī (last semester) zhè xuéqī (this semester) xià xuéqī (next semester)
Year (nián) ⚠️ No shàng/xià! qùnián (last year) jīnnián (this year) míngnián (next year)
Day (tiān) ⚠️ No shàng/xià! zuótiān (yesterday) jīntiān / xiànzài (today / now) míngtiān (tomorrow)
⚠️ CRUCIAL RULE: "shàng" (upper/previous) and "xià" (lower/next) CANNOT be used with Year (nián) or Day (tiān)!

🌍 "Nǎ guó rén" vs "Nǎr rén" Comparison
1. Nǎ guó rén? (Which nationality?) -> Answer: [Country] + rén
e.g., Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? -> Wǒ shì Měiguó rén / Jiānádà rén. (American / Canadian)
2. Nǎr rén? (Where are you from / Hometown?) -> Answer: [City/Region] + rén
e.g., Tā shì nǎr rén? -> Tā shì Běijīng rén / Niǔyuē rén. (From Beijing / From New York)

💡 Grammar Supplement 1: "#19 guò" & "... de shíhou"
1. "guò" (to celebrate): guò shēngri = celebrate a birthday.
2. "... de shíhou" (when... / during...):
e.g., Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma?
(Do Chinese people eat cake WHEN celebrating birthdays?)
⚠️ Distinction: "shénme shíhou" is a Question Word (When?), whereas "... de shíhou" is a Time Clause (When doing something...).
💡 Grammar Supplement 2: "zhùhè" vs "zhù"
• zhùhè (to congratulate on an occasion/event): Verb + Event
e.g., Wǒmen zhùhè tā de shēngri. (We congratulate his birthday.)
• zhù (to wish someone something): Verb + Person + Wish
e.g., Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Wish you a happy birthday!)

Text 1: Age & Party Details (Lines 9–17)
9. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Xīngqīrì shì shí yuè èrshíqī hào, shì Sòng Huá de shēngri.(Sunday is October 27th, it is Song Hua's birthday.)
10. Lín Nà: Shì ma? Tā jīnnián duō dà?(Really? How old is he this year?)
11. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Sòng Huá 1982 nián shí yuè èrshíqī rì chūshēng, shǔ gǒu. Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.(Song Hua was born on Oct 27, 1982, in the year of the dog. He is 20.)
12. Lín Nà: Tā shì nǎr rén?(Where is he from?)
13. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Tā shì Běijīng rén. Tā bàba, māma dōu zài Běijīng. Xīngqīrì xiàwǔ wǒmen yǒu yí ge jùhuì, zhùhè tā de shēngri. Lìbō, Dàwéi dōu qù, nǐ cānjiā bu cānjiā?(He is from Beijing. His dad and mom are both in Beijing. Sunday afternoon we have a party to celebrate his birthday. Libo and Dawei are both going, are you participating?)
14. Lín Nà: Tài hǎo le! Wǒ dāngrán cānjiā. Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma?(Great! Of course I will participate. Do Chinese people eat cake when celebrating birthdays?)
15. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Chī dàngāo.(Yes, eat cake.)
16. Lín Nà: Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma?(I'll buy a big cake, okay?)
17. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Hǎo a. Wǒ mǎi liǎng píng hóng pútaojiǔ.(Okay. I'll buy two bottles of red wine.)

Text 2: Celebrating the Birthday Party (Lines 1–9)
1. Lín Nà: Sòng Huá, zhè shì shēngri dàngāo. Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè!(Song Hua, this is the birthday cake. Happy birthday!)
2. Sòng Huá: Xièxie. Dàngāo zhēn piàoliang. Nǐmen lái, wǒ hěn gāoxìng.(Thank you. The cake is really beautiful. You all coming makes me very happy.)
3. Mǎ Dàwéi: Jīntiān wǒmen chī Běijīng kǎoyā. Wǒ hěn xǐhuan chī kǎoyā.(Today we eat Beijing roast duck. I really like eating roast duck.)
4. Dīng Lìbō: Wǒmen hē shénme jiǔ?(What wine are we drinking?)
5. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Dāngrán hē hóng pútaojiǔ, wǒmen hái chī shòumiàn.(Of course we drink red wine, we also eat longevity noodles.)
6. Lín Nà: Chī shòumiàn? Zhēn yǒu yìsi.(Eat longevity noodles? Really interesting.)
7. Sòng Huá: Lín Nà, nǐ de shēngri shì nǎ tiān?(Lin Na, which day is your birthday?)
8. Lín Nà: Shíyī yuè shí'èr hào.(November 12th.)
9. Sòng Huá: Hǎo, shíyī yuè shí'èr hào wǒmen zài lái chī shòumiàn.(Okay, on Nov 12th we will come again to eat longevity noodles.)
