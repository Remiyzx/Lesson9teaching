Grammar 1: Expressing Dates (BIG to SMALL) (Pg 141)

Rule: Always arrange time units from largest to smallest: Year -> Month -> Day -> Day of Week -> Time of Day.

✔ 2026 nián, shíyuè, èrshíqī hào, xīngqīsì, shàngwǔ
✘ Thursday morning, October 27th, 2026 (Never use English order!)
🎯 Key Q&A Reinforcement: Asking Dates
Q1: Tā nǎ nián chūshēng? (Which year was he born?)
A: Tā 1982 nián chūshēng. (He was born in 1982.)
Q2: Jīntiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? (What date is today?)
A: Jīntiān shì shíyuè èrshíqī hào. (Today is October 27th.)
Q3: Nǐ de shēngri shì nǎ tiān? (When/which day is your birthday?)
A: Wǒ de shēngri shì bāyuè èrshíyī hào. (My birthday is August 21st.)
Q4: Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ? (What day of the week is tomorrow?)
A: Míngtiān shì xīngqīsì. (Tomorrow is Thursday.)

⏰ Time Structure & Concept Table ("shàng" vs "xià")
Specific Time Order Formula:
[Day] + [Time Period] + [Hour] + [Minute]
e.g., Míngtiān shàngwǔ 9 diǎn (Tomorrow morning at 9 o'clock)
Comparative Table: Past/Present/Future Time Expressions
Time Unit Previous (shàng / 过去) Current (zhè / jīn / 现在) Next (xià / míng / 未来)
Month (yuè) shàng ge yuè (last month) zhè ge yuè (this month) xià ge yuè (next month)
Week (xīngqī) shàng xīngqī (last week) zhè xīngqī (this week) xià xīngqī (next week)
Year (nián) ⚠️ No shàng/xià! qùnián (last year) jīnnián (this year) míngnián (next year)
Day (tiān) ⚠️ No shàng/xià! zuótiān (yesterday) jīntiān / xiànzài (today / now) míngtiān (tomorrow)
🎯 Key Q&A Reinforcement: Asking "When" & "What Time"
💡 Difference: Use shénme shíhou for broad/general time (day, week, year). Use jǐ diǎn specifically for exact clock time (hours).
Q1: Nǐ shénme shíhou yǒu kè? (When do you have class?)
A: Wǒ míngtiān shàngwǔ yǒu kè. (I have class tomorrow morning.)
Q2: Nǐ shàngwǔ jǐ diǎn yǒu kè? (What time do you have class in the morning?)
A: Wǒ shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn yǒu kè. (I have class at 9 o'clock in the morning.)

🌍 "Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?" vs "Nǐ shì nǎr rén?" Comparison
1. Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? (Which nationality are you?) -> Answer: [Country] + rén
e.g., Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? -> Wǒ shì Měiguó rén / Jiānádà rén. (American / Canadian)
2. Nǐ shì nǎr rén? (Where are you from / Hometown?) -> Answer: [City/Region] + rén
e.g., Tā shì nǎr rén? -> Tā shì Běijīng rén / Shābā rén. (From Beijing / From Sabah)

💡 Grammar Supplement 1: "#19 guò" & "... de shíhou"
1. "guò" (to celebrate): guò shēngri = celebrate a birthday.

2. "... de shíhou" (when... / during...):
• Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma? (Do Chinese people eat cake WHEN celebrating birthdays?)
• Mèimei chànggē de shíhou hěn kě'ài. (WHEN younger sister sings, she is very cute.)
• Chī hànbǎo de shíhou wǒ yào hē kělè. (WHEN eating a hamburger, I want to drink cola.)
⚠️ Distinction: "shénme shíhou" is a Question Word (When?), whereas "... de shíhou" is a Time Clause (When doing something...).

Text 1: Age & Party Details (Lines 9–17)
9. (Sunday is October 27th, it is Song Hua's birthday.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Xīngqīrì shì shíyuè èrshíqī hào, shì Sòng Huá de shēngri.
10. (Really? How old is he this year?)
Lín Nà: Shì ma? Tā jīnnián duō dà?
11. (Song Hua was born on Oct 27, 1982, in the year of the dog. He is 20.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Sòng Huá yī jiǔ bā èr nián shíyuè èrshíqī rì chūshēng, shǔ gǒu. Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
12. (Where is he from?)
Lín Nà: Tā shì nǎr rén?
13. (He is from Beijing. His dad and mom are both in Beijing. Sunday afternoon we have a party to celebrate his birthday. Libo and Dawei are both going, are you participating?)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Tā shì Běijīng rén. Tā bàba, māma dōu zài Běijīng. Xīngqīrì xiàwǔ wǒmen yǒu yí ge jùhuì, zhùhè tā de shēngri. Lìbō, Dàwéi dōu qù, nǐ cānjiā bu cānjiā?
14. (Great! Of course I will participate. Do Chinese people eat cake when celebrating birthdays?)
Lín Nà: Tài hǎo le! Wǒ dāngrán cānjiā. Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma?
15. (Yes, eat cake.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Chī dàngāo.
16. (I'll buy a big cake, okay?)
Lín Nà: Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma?
17. (Okay. I'll buy two bottles of red wine.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Hǎo a. Wǒ mǎi liǎng píng hóng pútaojiǔ.

💡 Grammar Supplement 2: "zhùhè" vs "zhù"
• zhùhè (congratulate on an event): Verb + Event ➔ e.g., Wǒmen zhùhè tā de shēngri. (We congratulate his birthday.)
• zhù (wish someone something): Verb + Person + Wish ➔ e.g., Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Wish you a happy birthday!)

Text 2: Celebrating the Birthday Party (Lines 1–9)
1. (Song Hua, this is the birthday cake. Happy birthday!)
Lín Nà: Sòng Huá, zhè shì shēngri dàngāo. Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè!
2. (Thank you. The cake is really beautiful. You all coming makes me very happy.)
Sòng Huá: Xièxie. Dàngāo zhēn piàoliang. Nǐmen lái, wǒ hěn gāoxìng.
3. (Today we eat Beijing roast duck. I really like eating roast duck.)
Mǎ Dàwéi: Jīntiān wǒmen chī Běijīng kǎoyā. Wǒ hěn xǐhuan chī kǎoyā.
4. (What wine are we drinking?)
Dīng Lìbō: Wǒmen hē shénme jiǔ?
5. (Of course we drink red wine, we also eat longevity noodles.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Dāngrán hē hóng pútaojiǔ, wǒmen hái chī shòumiàn.
6. (Eat longevity noodles? Really interesting.)
Lín Nà: Chī shòumiàn? Zhēn yǒu yìsi.
7. (Lin Na, which day is your birthday?)
Sòng Huá: Lín Nà, nǐ de shēngri shì nǎ tiān?
8. (November 12th.)
Lín Nà: Shíyīyuè shí'èr hào.
9. (Okay, on Nov 12th we will come again to eat longevity noodles.)
Sòng Huá: Hǎo, shíyīyuè shí'èr hào wǒmen zài lái chī shòumiàn.
